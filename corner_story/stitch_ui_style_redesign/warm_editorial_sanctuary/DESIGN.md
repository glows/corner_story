## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the thoughtful stillness of slow journalism and independent print periodicals (Kinfolk, Cereal, Monocle). It translates the tactile elegance of art paper, quiet coffee shops, and brutalist architecture into an intimate digital space for discovering neighborhood haunts, natural wine cellars, and hidden galleries.

### Character & Tone
- **Curated & Discerning:** Unhurried, deliberate visual cadence. Whitespace is an active architectural element rather than vacant room.
- **Literary & Tactile:** Rich typography pairings that simulate fine editorial print; organic paper-inspired surfaces that reject synthetic gloss.
- **Understated Precision:** 1px hairline rules, delicate framing, and restrained micro-interactions convey craftsmanship over flashiness.

### Aesthetic Direction
An elevated blend of **Minimalism** and **Tactile Editorial Layout**:
- Ample negative space guiding visual focus effortlessly toward high-resolution architectural photography.
- High typographic contrast between literary serifs and neutral, highly-legible grotesque interface labels.
- Pill forms for active, navigational touch targets set against expansive rectangular frames and editorial grids.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic restraint of uncoated rag paper, dried ink, and terracotta stoneware. It rejects aggressive saturated primaries in favor of deep soot, washed linen, and an earthy sienna accent.

### Palette Matrix
- **Primary (`#1A1A18`):** Deep Soot. Primary typographic driver, active structural outlines, and key interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#6E6B65`):** Warm Graphite. Curatorial metadata, subtext, captions, and structural secondary accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C85A32`):** Burnt Terracotta. Applied selectively to verified badges, curated neighborhood tags, active filter pills, and critical calls-to-action.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5`):** Eggshell Linen. Canvas foundation that eliminates stark, clinical digital glare.
- **Surface Auxiliary (`#FFFFFF`):** Gallery White. Elevated card tiles, contextual overlays, and modal containers.
- **Tonal Contrast (`#F2EFE9`):** Washed Paper. Secondary input fields, chip inactive fills, and recessed layout bands.
- **Border Hairline (`#E6E2DA`):** Raw Fiber. 1px ultra-crisp structural rules and card boundaries.

### Application Rules
- Maintain an 80/15/5 distribution rule: 80% natural neutral canvas/surfaces, 15% rich soot typography and framing, 5% burnt terracotta accents.
- Never use pure black (`#000000`). Text must always sit in deep charcoal soot to honor editorial paper tones.

## Typography

The type scale contrasts classical editorial serif headings with rational, humanist sans-serif utility typography.

### Hierarchy & Stylistic Behavior
- **Serif Voice (`Newsreader`):** Used across display and titles. Evokes literary journals and historic broadsheets. Apply font-style italics for neighborhood monographs, curator quotes, place descriptors, and architectural highlights.
- **Sans-Serif Functional Voice (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Used for informative body copy, operational data, metadata tags, and navigational labels.
- **Micro-Caps (`label-caps`):** Micro-labels, category tags, street names, and distance calculations use uppercase transformation with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic Monocle-style data tables.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric, gallery-inspired editorial grid that honors whitespace as an active compositional tool.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem / 20px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Single-column editorial stream with asymmetric horizontal card carousels overflowing the canvas margin.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `margin` (2.5rem / 40px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Dual-column editorial storytelling with alternating full-width hero photography.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with a constrained maximum container width of `1280px` centered within `margin-desktop`. Column gutters scale to `gutter-lg` (2.5rem / 40px) to maintain a spacious, publication-grade layout.

### Rhythm & Proportions
- Spacing relies on the `8px` baseline rhythm. Macro-sections separate distinct editorial features using `space-2xl` (64px) on mobile and `space-3xl` (96px) on desktop.
- Inline components (metadata, avatars, badges) are governed strictly by tight `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-blur, heavy drop shadows, relying instead on clean tonal layers, crisp 1px borders, and ultra-diffused atmospheric lift.

### Dimensional Stratification
- **Ground (Level 0):** Canvas base `#FBF9F5` (Eggshell Linen). Zero shadow, 0px border.
- **Surface Layer (Level 1):** Flat cards, panels, and sidebars in `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px hairline border in `#E6E2DA`. Depth is established purely through surface tonality and hairline definition.
- **Floating Hover & Interactive (Level 2):** Hovered gallery cards and selected venues gain a faint ambient warmth:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(26, 26, 24, 0.05), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(26, 26, 24, 0.03);`
  Border transitions to `#DCD7CE`.
- **Overlay & Modal Sheet (Level 3):** Curated neighborhood drawer, filter sheets, and contextual bottom menus:
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 26, 24, 0.08);`
  Includes a soft frosted backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.88);`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines smooth pill contours with refined architectural card radiuses, maintaining a warm tactile atmosphere.

### Radius System
- **Pill Foundations (`9999px`):** Used universally across buttons, status tags, badge filters, search inputs, and chips. The complete pill brings soft organic contrast against angular imagery.
- **Large Editorial Containers (`rounded-xl` / 24px–32px):** Used for photography card frames, modal drawers, and map preview windows. Cards feature generous inner corner radiuses to frame photography like modern archival prints.
- **Micro-Containers (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Applied to thumbnail imagery, inline tooltips, and compact metric boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background `#1A1A18`, text `#FBF9F5`, font `label-md`. Height 48px, horizontal padding 24px, pill roundedness (`9999px`). Hover shifts smoothly to `rgba(26, 26, 24, 0.88)` with a 1px scale transition.
- **Secondary Pill:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border in `#E6E2DA`, text `#1A1A18`. On hover, background shifts to `#F2EFE9` and border to `#1A1A18`.
- **Terracotta Accent Action:** Background `#C85A32`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-md`. Used exclusively for critical calls-to-action (e.g., "Reserve Table", "Save to Guide").
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, text `#1A1A18`, 1px bottom border underline on hover, 0px border radius.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Inactive:** Height 36px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E6E2DA`, text `#6E6B65`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Active:** Background `#1A1A18`, border 1px solid `#1A1A18`, text `#FBF9F5`.
- **Editorial Category Badges:** Background `rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.2)`, text `#C85A32`, typography `label-caps`.

### Place & Discovery Cards
- **Curated Guide Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E6E2DA`, border radius `1.5rem` (24px), internal padding 16px.
- **Photography Treatment:** Aspect ratio 4:5 or 16:11, corner radius `1rem` (16px), subtle inner overlay gradient for legibility if text over image. Imagery maintains a warm, filmic color-grade.
- **Metadata Grouping:** Place category in `label-caps` (`#6E6B65`), venue title in `headline-sm` with optional italicized neighborhood tag, subtle price tier indicator (`$$$`) in soot.

### Lists & City Directories
- Clean horizontal list row with 0px card borders, separated by 1px solid `#E6E2DA` dividers.
- Row padding: 20px top and bottom.
- Monocle-style numbered indexes (e.g., "01", "02") set in `Newsreader` italic, color `#6E6B65`, right-padded by 24px.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Global Search:** Height 52px, pill shape (`9999px`), background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E6E2DA`, text `#1A1A18`, placeholder `#6E6B65`. Focus state introduces a crisp 1px soot ring (`#1A1A18`).
- **Form Fields:** Height 44px, background `#F2EFE9`, border 1px solid transparent, border radius 12px. Focus shifts background to `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#1A1A18`.

### Selection Controls
- **Radio & Checkbox:** 20px diameter/width, 1px border `#6E6B65`. Checked state features background `#1A1A18` with pure white `#FFFFFF` tick or dot.
- **Toggle Switch:** 44px length by 24px height, track background `#E6E2DA` transitioning to `#1A1A18` on active, circular white thumb with Level 1 elevation.
